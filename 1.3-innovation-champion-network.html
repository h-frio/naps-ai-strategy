Operating model
Federated champion architecture

The network runs on a three-layer federated model: a central owner defining standards, topic-based champions guiding by capability, and site champions translating capability into local business reality.

🏛️

Central Network Owner

Owns the playbook, nominations, onboarding, cadences, training alignment, and overall health of the champion community across all sites and domains.

🎯

Topic Champions

Domain experts who guide tool selection, share best practices, triage ideas, and support peers before any opportunity becomes a formal intake submission.

🏭

Site Champions

Named contacts at each NAPS location who understand local processes, pain points, and adoption realities across seed manufacturing, site support, and product support.
