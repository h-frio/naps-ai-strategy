Our approach
Five-phase AI strategy framework

We organized our work into a five-phase model aligned to the validated strategy approach — awareness, intake, alignment, rapid POC, and scale & support. Each phase below consolidates workstreams, governance, and delivery assets into a cleaner enterprise narrative.

Phase 1
Organizational Awareness
Build shared understanding, local sponsorship, and AI literacy so teams can identify meaningful opportunities with clear business ownership.
Phase 2
Idea Intake & Review
Capture ideas through a unified intake path, make review criteria transparent, and route each demand to the right level of rigor.
Phase 3
Solution Alignment
Evaluate feasibility, classify complexity, define decision rights, and align the opportunity to measurable value and the right delivery path.
Phase 4
Rapid POC Development
Convert selected opportunities into structured proofs of concept with fit-for-purpose teams, sprint delivery, and explicit quality gates.
Phase 5
Scale & Support
Scale validated use cases, sustain adoption, monitor impact, and reinforce value realization through support and continuous improvement.
Phase 1 — Organizational Awareness
Builds shared understanding, local sponsorship, and AI literacy so teams can identify meaningful opportunities with clear business ownership.
Communication Campaigns →

Quarterly campaigns, storytelling, and intranet visibility to explain what AI is relevant for NAPS and where employees should start.

Digital Literacy Trail →

Role-based literacy path for generative AI, agentic AI, automation, analytics, and responsible use across operations teams.

Innovation Champion Network →

Site and function champions coach peers, shape early demand, and help ideas arrive with stronger problem framing and ownership.

Use Case Library

Searchable examples, templates, lessons learned, and internal references to accelerate reuse and reduce duplicate experimentation.

Phase 2 — Idea Intake & Review
Captures ideas through a unified intake path, makes review criteria transparent, and routes each demand to the right level of rigor.
Smart Intake Form →

Structured intake captures requester information, problem statement, expected impact, and solution signals in one enterprise entry point.

Initial Triage

Fast triage validates sponsor, pain point, category, and minimum business case before advancing the idea into full review.

Phase 3 — Solution Alignment
Evaluates feasibility, classifies complexity, defines decision rights, and aligns the opportunity to measurable value and the right delivery path.
Innovation Board Review →

Monthly cross-functional review aligns IT, Legal, Compliance, Finance, and business representatives on decisions, escalations, and feedback loops to rejected or reframed ideas.

Opportunity Classification →

Each initiative is classified into Quick Wins, Core Innovations, or Strategic Bets to define delivery path, team model, and approvals.

Portfolio Visibility →

Shared visibility by status, business unit, site, and technology type supports demand transparency and smarter resourcing decisions.

Phase 4 — Rapid POC Development
Converts selected opportunities into structured proofs of concept with fit-for-purpose teams, sprint delivery, and explicit quality gates.
Delivery Track

Approved initiatives are handed off to execution teams and tracked across all development stages — from kickoff through build, review, and go-live — with full visibility by tier, status, and responsible team.

Phase 5 — Scale & Support
Scales validated use cases, sustains adoption, monitors impact, and reinforces value realization through support and continuous improvement.
Adoption Monitoring

Leading indicators — such as active users, engagement rates, and behavior change signals — are tracked continuously to ensure scaled solutions gain traction and remain relevant across sites and functions.

Value Realization

Lagging KPIs quantify the business impact of delivered use cases, including financial benefits, hours saved, error reduction, and throughput gains — providing the evidence base for sustained investment and program credibility.

Support & Continuous Improvement

A structured support model defines how teams report issues, request enhancements, and escalate incidents for live use cases — with clear ownership across L1 triage, L2 functional support, and L3 technical resolution.

Recognition & Success Stories →

Contributors are celebrated, wins are published, and successful approaches are packaged as reusable references — reinforcing a culture of innovation and accelerating adoption across additional sites and business units.

Governance structure
Three levels of governance

The NAPS AI governance model operates at three levels — operational, review, and steering — each with clear decision rights, escalation paths, and RACI accountability.

Governance levels

1

CoE Operational Layer

Day-to-day intake management, triage, coaching, and delivery support. Manages Phase 1–2 execution and Tier 1 delivery directly.

2

Innovation Board Review

Monthly cross-functional governance body. Scores submissions, classifies tiers, allocates resources, and returns structured feedback to all submitters.

3

Steering Committee

Executive-level oversight for Tier 3 bets, major investment decisions, escalations, and program-level reporting on KPIs and portfolio health.

Success measurement
Five KPI dimensions

Program performance is tracked across five dimensions that connect capability growth, delivery velocity, adoption depth, business value, and responsible use compliance.

Dimension 1
Literacy
% of NAPS employees completing L1 Digital Literacy Trail modules. Target: 100% all employees.
Dimension 2
Pipeline
Volume and quality of ideas entering and advancing through the intake and review stages each quarter.
Dimension 3
Delivery
Number of approved POCs reaching go-live per quarter, by tier. Tracks velocity and delivery health.
Dimension 4
Value
Quantified business impact from delivered use cases — time saved, cost reduced, quality improved, risk reduced.
Dimension 5
Compliance
% of active use cases compliant with Responsible AI, data privacy, and governance policies. Zero tolerance for critical violations.
