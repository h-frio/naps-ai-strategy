Step 1 · Request setup

Capture the core ownership details.

Start with the submitter, process owner, sponsor, and the title of the work item. This makes the intake easier to route and triage.

Who owns the work?Clarify who submitted the request and who owns the process.
Who sponsors it?Capture executive support or record N/A if none exists yet.
What is the scope?Name the primary unit and estimate the population using the data.
Leave blank if the submitter is also the process owner.
