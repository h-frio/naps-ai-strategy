Recognition model
Two linked tracks support sustained adoption
Following the Pathfinders model, NAPS recognizes both the growth of AI capability and the business outcomes created with that capability. The structure below keeps the language aligned to the existing NAPS AI Strategy while preserving a simple, transparent operating model.
Visibility model
Recognition should reinforce scale, not stop at celebration
In Phase 5, recognition is not a side activity. It is part of the reinforcement system that sustains adoption, creates role models, and turns successful delivery into reusable evidence for the broader portfolio.

Where recognition becomes visible

  • Portfolio visibility: badge progression, award nominations, and published wins can be linked to the portfolio view for stronger evidence of adoption maturity.
  • Community storytelling: Pathfinders and NAPS AI channels can showcase who progressed, what they built, and what measurable outcome was achieved.
  • Leadership reporting: success stories help Innovation Board and sponsors see not only what shipped, but what is scaling and where behavior changed.

Phase 5 operating principles

  • Capability and impact stay linked: learning badges alone are not value proof, and value awards alone do not show repeatable skill development.
  • Recognition follows evidence: nominations should reference delivery results, responsible use, reuse potential, and business ownership.
  • Stories feed the system: every recognized win should generate a reusable success story, example, or playbook for the next sites and teams.
The intent is to make AI growth visible, aspirational, and fair while staying aligned with NAPS AI Strategy governance and language.

What a Phase 5 recognition story should look like
To stay useful, every recognition example should connect a person or team, a delivered solution, a measurable outcome, and a reason the story matters for broader NAPS scale-up.

Track 1 · Learning Milestones
AI Belt Badges — NAPS AI Strategy Context

This track adapts the Pathfinders recognition concept to the NAPS AI Strategy language. Each belt is tied to the Digital Literacy Trail and should be used to show capability progression that supports long-term adoption in Phase 5.

White Belt
AI Everyday User
Level 1 · Digital Literacy Trail
Requirements
  • ✓Complete all L1 modules and core labs.
  • ✓Pass the foundational knowledge check.
  • ✓Demonstrate safe, policy-aligned use of AI in daily work.
Recommended recognition: e-Card for first visible AI learning milestones and peer encouragement.
Yellow Belt
AI Citizen Developer
Level 2 · Digital Literacy Trail
Requirements
  • ✓Complete all L2 modules and hands-on labs.
  • ✓Deliver one low-code or no-code improvement for a team workflow.
  • ✓Document the solution following governance and handoff standards.
Recommended recognition: Bronze-level impact nomination for local, repeatable productivity improvement.
Green Belt
AI Power User
Level 3 · Digital Literacy Trail
Requirements
  • ✓Complete L3 advanced content and project-based learning.
  • ✓Co-lead an AI solution with measurable benefit across multiple teams or sites.
  • ✓Share learnings through the Pathfinders and NAPS AI community.
Recommended recognition: Silver-level impact nomination for cross-functional outcomes with evidence.
Black Belt
AI Product Developer
Level 4 · Digital Literacy Trail
Requirements
  • ✓Complete L4 specialization and relevant technical certifications.
  • ✓Lead a Tier 3 strategic AI solution as product or technical lead.
  • ✓Mentor other builders and support scale-up across the network.
Recommended recognition: Gold-level impact nomination for enterprise-scale value with recurring benefit.
Belts acknowledge capability growth. They should only be awarded when learning is combined with practical application, responsible use, and visible contribution to adoption.
Track 2 · Impact Outcomes
Impact Awards — NAPS AI Strategy Context

This track keeps the existing recognition framework and salary-percentage logic intact, while adding AI-specific examples and shared NAPS language. The goal is to make AI outcomes visible without creating a separate recognition system outside the established model.

e-Card
Everyday Excellence
Peer recognition · no salary element
Typical belt link: White Belt
Recognize curiosity, peer support, and responsible AI behaviors that create momentum in the earliest stages of adoption.
  • →Helping colleagues use safe prompts and approved tools correctly.
  • →Sharing a useful prompt library, template, or small workflow tip with the team.
  • →Organizing local AI learning circles, demos, or show-and-tell moments.
Bronze
Local Impact
0.2–1 salary
Typical belt link: Yellow Belt
Recognize local, repeatable AI-driven improvements that save time, improve quality, or remove manual friction for a team or site.
  • →A low-code automation that removes weekly manual reporting effort.
  • →A workflow assistant that reduces repetitive approvals or handoffs.
  • →An AI-supported SOP or quality process improvement with verified local benefit.
Silver
Cross-Functional Impact
2–3 salary
Typical belt link: Green Belt
Recognize multi-team or multi-site AI solutions with measurable benefits and stronger evidence of reuse beyond one local context.
  • →A dashboard copilot or assistant that shortens decision cycles across several sites.
  • →A reusable automation pattern that reduces manual entry errors in multiple functions.
  • →An AI-enabled quality alert solution adopted across several operating groups.
Gold
Enterprise Game-Changer
5–25 salary
Typical belt link: Black Belt
Recognize strategic AI solutions aligned with NAPS and Bayer priorities that create recurring, material impact at enterprise scale.
  • →A Tier 3 AI product that materially reduces cycle time, cost, safety risk, or quality loss.
  • →A platform capability or agentic workflow standard adopted broadly across the organization.
  • →A high-value strategic bet that becomes a repeatable model for the portfolio.
Use the existing approval path and ranges already defined in the broader recognition framework. NAPS AI Strategy adds AI-specific examples, sponsor language, and success-story packaging for scale.
